Case studies

Five systems I designed and own at PayPal — described here at the architecture level, without proprietary code or internal specifics.

AI-powered code review at scale

hours → minutes

1Problem

Manual PR review was a bottleneck — every pull request needed a senior engineer's time, competing with everything else on their plate.

2Approach

Designed and architected an AI-powered Pull Request Analyzer using LLMs to review code, generate contextual comments, flag defects, and recommend improvements before merge.

3Outcome

Cut review effort from hours to minutes. Paired with executive dashboards giving leadership visibility into review quality and adoption org-wide.

GenAI test generation with a governed RAG layer

+80% coverage

1Problem

Writing functional tests from a requirement or an API spec was slow and inconsistent across engineers, and didn't scale with delivery pace.

2Approach

Built a GPT-4-based platform generating tests and eval cases from a requirement or spec, layered with a RAG pipeline indexing the existing test repository so output matches established conventions.

3Outcome

Lifted test coverage 80%, cut manual testing effort 40%.

Two-sided AI quality gates — before and after release

pre + post release

1Problem

Catching quality issues at only one point in the lifecycle leaves a gap — regressions introduced late, or bad data slipping through right after launch.

2Approach

Built two complementary gates: a pre-release tool comparing candidate builds against production metrics, and alpha testing — owned outright by my team — auditing every new model/feature for data-quality issues right after release.

3Outcome

Regressions caught pre-release; production data validated for integrity before it's ever used in a real business decision.

Model lifecycle governance & cost optimization

$1M+/yr

1Problem

Legacy models, features, and datasets accumulate indefinitely without a retirement process, quietly driving up infrastructure cost.

2Approach

Led the retirement and optimization of legacy models, features, and datasets, instituting governance discipline over lifecycle decisions instead of letting them run by default.

3Outcome

Delivered more than $1M in annual infrastructure and operational cost savings.

Building a quality engineering organization from zero

0 → 10, 4 promos

1Problem

No dedicated quality engineering function existed to support a growing enterprise AI/ML platform organization.

2Approach

Built the North America QE org from the ground up — hiring for ownership, problem-solving, and collaboration — and grew it through structured coaching across four global locations.

3Outcome

10 engineers, 4 promotions to senior/staff/manager, zero regretted attrition over 8 years — including growing one engineer into a manager role and continuing to manage her team for over a year.
